A simple, self-custodial Bitcoin wallet.

One screen. Your balance, Send and Receive. Nothing more. No charts, no news, no exchange, no unnecessary clutter. Everything else stays tucked away in settings.

Your keys stay on your phone, protected by your PIN. Back up your wallet once with 12 words. Choose your transaction fee: Slow, Medium or Fast.

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Free and open source.

Simple idea. Full control.
